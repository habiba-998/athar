## Reading this

- The set of attack paths is fixed by the graph; only their *scores and ranking* depend on the weights.
- τ-b of 1.0 means the ordering is identical; values around 0.75–0.85 mean moderate re-ordering between similar paths.
- The stability of the **#1 recommendation** measures whether the *structural* conclusion (which relationship is the chokepoint) survives different weights. That conclusion comes from the graph, so it is expected to be more robust than the numbers.
- **Absolute values are assumption-dependent.** The posture index and the identity of the single highest-scoring path can move noticeably with the weights (see the ranges above). Compare scores only within one scoring configuration, treat them as a prioritisation aid rather than a measurement, and calibrate the weights on real data before relying on absolute values.
- Robustness here is a property of two synthetic environments; it is not evidence that the weights match real attacker behaviour.
